## Schemes

- **Scheme A, CTOR_COMMITS_OWNER**: the owner (and for Argent the guardian) is inside `keccak256(constructorInput)`, so the CREATE2 address itself commits to the owner. A claimant reveals (factory, salt, bytecodeHash, ctor input); the BSC-side verifier recomputes the address and checks an owner signature. Covers Argent (both factories), 0x3f4f6134..., 0x4b04200d... Verified 25/25 on each. Argent also needs the guardian value, which is in the ctor, so a recovery proof reveals it too.
- **Scheme B, ERA_STATE_OWNER**: nothing in salt or ctor identifies the owner (Clave). The owner (passkey secp256r1 pubkey) lives in the initializer calldata/account storage on Era. A verifier on BSC cannot recompute it from the address; it needs an attested read of Era state (or a proof of the deploy tx input plus a passkey signature). The initializer is not committed by the address, so a preimage alone proves nothing. Hypothesis, not tested.
- **Scheme C, SAFE_INITIALIZER_PREIMAGE**: salt = keccak(keccak(initializer) || saltNonce). Claimant reveals initializer (owners, threshold) and saltNonce, verifier recomputes the address and checks a signature from one of the revealed owners. Current owners can differ from initial owners.

## Caveats and what was not verified

- Counts are ContractDeployed events from system contract 0x8006 with topic1 = factory, over blocks 0..head (Era head 72,637,244-72,637,382 at query time, Abstract head 88,257,046), with adaptive splitting at the RPC 10,000-result limit; no failed ranges. "Complete" means no range failed, not an independent cross-check against another indexer. Distinct-address counts were checked only for AGW(Era), Clave V1/V2, Safe; not checked for the others or Abstract.
- Samples: AGW Era 20 and Abstract 24, Clave 25 each, Argent 25 each, Safe 14, 0x3f4f 25, 0x4b04 25; first 3 events plus random events. 25/25 is not proof for all accounts (a factory upgrade or alternate entry point could change the pattern).
- "Random" salt for Clave and Argent is a hypothesis from the absence of any tested relation; it cannot be proven.
- Owner binding is to the INITIAL owner only; current owners were not read for any wallet.
- Factory discovery for "other popular" accounts: top deployers by ContractDeployed counts in four 300k-block windows (recent, ~60.0M, ~45.0M, ~30.0M) on Era. Factories active only in other windows can be missed. Contract names come from the Era block explorer API (verified source only); brands for unverified factories were not identified.
- ZKsync SSO mainnet: no deployment found; docs only list testnet. Holdstation: factory not found.
- Other chains for Clave, Argent, Safe, 0x3f4f/0x4b04: unmeasured (only Era checked; AGW also on Abstract).
- Only AGW (Era and Abstract) uses SALT_IS_KECCAK_OF_INITIAL_OWNER among the factories examined.
